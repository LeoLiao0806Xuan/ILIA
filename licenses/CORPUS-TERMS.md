# International-law corpus terms

ILIA's source code is licensed under Apache-2.0. That licence does **not** grant
rights in third-party treaties, judicial decisions, translations, publications,
PDF layouts, logos, emblems, images or other corpus material.

ILIA is currently distributed free of charge as a non-commercial open-source
project. Each corpus item remains subject to its source institution's terms and
applicable law. The row-by-row review is recorded in
`corpus-redistribution-rights-matrix.csv`.

The next distributable corpus contains 49 ILIA-generated normalized plain-text
artifacts. It does not include the downloaded source PDFs. Each artifact states
that it is not an official edition and provides an official-source link and a
SHA-256 integrity record. The ICRC customary IHL rules study is excluded.

In particular:

- United Nations and United Nations Treaty Collection materials are not treated
  as carrying a general redistribution licence merely because they are publicly
  downloadable.
- ICRC treaty instruments are represented as normalized legal text with source
  attribution. The separately authored ICRC customary IHL rules study is not
  included in the distributable corpus.
- Red Cross and Red Crescent emblems are protected and are not licensed for use
  as ILIA branding.
- ICJ PDF editions are not treated as carrying an express worldwide
  redistribution grant.

No corpus item may be relicensed under Apache-2.0. Downstream redistributors are
responsible for satisfying the applicable source terms and obtaining any
permission required for their intended use.
